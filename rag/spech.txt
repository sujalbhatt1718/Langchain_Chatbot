## Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish

Good morning everyone,

Life is not always going to go according to your plan. You will face failures, rejection, confusion, and moments when you feel that you are not good enough.

But remember—**your failures do not define you. How you respond to them does.**

Sometimes, you won't understand why something is happening in your life. A rejection may feel like the end. A failure may feel like a waste of time. But later, you may realize that those experiences were preparing you for something much better.

So, **trust the journey.**

Don't spend your life trying to become someone else. Find what you love, work hard at it, and keep learning.

You have only one life. Don't live it according to someone else's expectations.

Have the courage to follow your own path.

And when people tell you that something is impossible, don't immediately believe them. Maybe they are looking at your present while you are building your future.

Keep learning. Keep improving. Keep moving forward.

**Stay hungry. Stay foolish.**

Thank you.
